## What Happened in August

August 2026 wasn't all that busy for Pactus. What did happen was worth noting
though: **PIP-55 activated at block 8,000,000**, cutting the block reward from
1.0 PAC to 0.5 PAC per block, the network's first ever halving. A quiet,
clean, automatic milestone.

Contrary to this month, August has historically been one of the busiest months
on the Pactus calendar. Take a look.

## August In Review

**August 1, 2023** Pactus published the
[Story of Testnet-1](https://pactus.org/2023/08/01/testnet-1-concluded/), the
first real test of the network outside a lab environment.

**August 22, 2025** The community voted to pass
[PIP-43](https://pips.pactus.org/PIPs/pip-43), splitting block rewards
**70 percent to validators** and **30 percent to the Pactus Foundation**.

The vote pushed the network's reward sustainability out from 6 years to 10,
and by the end of that same month, the network had crossed
**4,500 active validators**.

The 70/30 split from 2025 is still running today. It is the same foundation
PIP-55 was built on top of this year, when the halving activated at block
8,000,000.

## PIP-55: The First Halving

**Activated August 2026, at block 8,000,000.** The Pactus network's first
halving went live automatically, with no chain split and no interruption. Under
[PIP-55](https://pips.pactus.org/PIPs/pip-55), the block reward schedule is:

| Block Height            | Block Reward |
| ----------------------- | -----------: |
| 1 – 8,000,000           |    1.000 PAC |
| 8,000,001 – 24,000,000  |    0.500 PAC |
| 24,000,001 – 56,000,000 |    0.250 PAC |
| 56,000,001 onward       |    0.125 PAC |

The 70/30 reward split between validators and the Pactus Foundation continues
unchanged at every stage.
